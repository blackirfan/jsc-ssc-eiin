# SSC/JSC Excel vs PDF Audit — Findings

Generated by `scripts/audit.py`. Ground truth = the 10,414 result PDFs in `pdfs/`,
using the rules from the `instructions` sheet:
- non-science = Business Studies + Humanities (combined)
- avg GPA = sum of GPAs of all examinees / total examinees (failed = GPA 0)

## Scope

- SSC: 6,648 PDFs checked against 6,663 `done` rows in the `ssc` sheet
- JSC: 3,766 PDFs checked against 3,766 `done` rows in the `jsc` sheet
- 10,414 (eiin, year) records compared in total
- **8,300 records (~80%) have at least one field that disagrees with the PDF**

Despite the large count, the disagreements are **not random** — they reduce to
three systemic, explainable causes plus a handful of one-off issues.

## Root cause 1 — "non-science" is not actually combined (biggest issue)

The `nonscience_*` columns almost never equal Business Studies + Humanities
combined. In every checked example, they equal just **one** of the two
groups (whichever the old scrape logic last wrote), and the other group's
students are silently missing from `nonscience_examinee_total/passed/gpa5`.

Example — `ssc_108019_2011.pdf`, EIIN 108019, SSC 2011:
| group | total | passed | gpa5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business Studies | 75 | 71 | 4 |
| Humanities | 23 | 21 | 0 |
| **Combined (correct nonscience)** | **98** | **92** | **4** |
| Excel `nonscience_*` | 75 | 71 | 4 |

Affected: **nonscience_examinee_total** 5,721 rows, **nonscience_examinee_passed** 5,682 rows.

For JSC, the single group is literally named "NONE" in the PDF. In 835 rows
the excel's `nonscience_examinee_total/passed` is stored as **0** even
though the PDF shows the full class (e.g. `jsc_107820_2018.pdf`: PDF total
397, excel 0) — i.e. the "NONE" group wasn't recognized as non-science at
all for those rows.

## Root cause 2 — group-level average GPA uses the wrong denominator

The instructions say avg GPA = sum / **total examinees**, with failed
students counted as GPA 0. The overall `avg_gpa` column does follow this
rule correctly. But `science_avg_gpa` and `nonscience_avg_gpa` do not — they
appear to divide by (group total − absentees) instead of the full group
total, and (for nonscience) sometimes sum across both non-science groups
while reporting only one group's total/passed. Net effect: the stored
group-level averages are inconsistent with both the stated rule and with
each other.

Example — same `ssc_108019_2011.pdf`, Science group (total 60, one absentee):
sum of GPAs = 235.32 → correct avg = 235.32 / 60 = **3.92**; excel stores
**3.99** (≈ 235.32 / 59, i.e. divides by total-minus-absent).

Affected: **science_avg_gpa** 907 rows, **nonscience_avg_gpa** 4,542 rows.

## Root cause 3 — Science group sometimes dropped entirely

In 1,262 SSC rows the PDF has a small (often single-digit) Science group
that the excel records as **0** across the board (`science_examinee_total`
1,262 rows, `science_examinee_passed` 1,241 rows), even though the group is
clearly printed in the PDF. Example: `ssc_107820_2011.pdf` has 4 Science
examinees; excel row shows 0.

## Minor / one-off issues

- **avg_gpa = 0.0 stored as blank** (3 rows: ssc 108496/2016, ssc & jsc
  134105/2015 & 2014) — when every examinee failed, sum is 0 so avg is
  legitimately 0.0, but excel stores a blank cell instead, indistinguishable
  from "no data."
- **15 PDFs missing** despite `scrape_status = done`: EIIN 108019 (SSC),
  every year except 2011 and 2020 (12 years), and EIIN 108487 SSC 2022. The
  excel rows/status exist but there is no PDF on disk to verify against —
  either the download was lost or the file needs to be re-fetched.
- **204 header/roll-data GPA-5 mismatches** and **67 "partially known" GPA
  records**, both confined to JSC 2011 files where only some students'
  individual GPA is printed (the rest show blank brackets). These are a
  PDF-format limitation, not treated as excel errors (excluded from the
  mismatch counts above).
- **0 orphan PDFs**: every PDF's `(eiin, year)` has a matching excel row,
  and every PDF's status is correctly `done` — no bookkeeping drift there.

## Files in this folder

- `mismatches.csv` — 19,358 rows, one per (kind, eiin, year, field) disagreement, with the excel value and the PDF-computed value
- `anomalies.csv` — parsing/data-availability notes (not excel errors)
- `missing_pdfs.csv` — the 15 `done` rows with no PDF on disk
- `group_names_seen.json` — sanity check of section headers seen (SCIENCE / BUSINESS STUDIES / HUMANITIES / NONE only — no surprises)
- `progress_log.txt` — timestamped run log

## Suggested next step

Root causes 1–3 are systematic and mechanically fixable straight from the
PDFs (the `pdf_value` column in `mismatches.csv` is already the corrected
number, per the instructions sheet's own rules). I have **not** modified
`excel/ssc_jsc_results_scraped.xlsx` — recommend confirming before any
bulk rewrite of ~8,300 rows.
